# Suno style prompts (instrumental, modern orchestral, uplifting, warm, non-repetitive)

Settings: Custom mode, Instrumental ON. Keep BPM and key in the same family across tracks (D major, 92–98 BPM). Ask for ~2:30–3:00 and expect ~3:00–3:20.

## Exclude (all tracks)

```text
vocals, singing, choir lyrics, rap, EDM drop, dubstep, trap hi-hats, heavy drums, distorted guitar, harsh synth lead, dark, sad, ominous, tense horror, repetitive loop, monotonous ostinato, lo-fi, chiptune
```

## Track A — opening (curious, gentle → warm)

```text
Instrumental modern orchestral, uplifting, warm, positive. Solo piano and soft harp open gently, then strings, flute and clarinet join with a hopeful melody. Light pizzicato and glockenspiel sparkle, warm cello undertone, subtle brass swell near the end. Soft modern percussion, brush kit and a gentle pulse, 94 BPM, D major. Compact arrangement of about two and a half minutes with a clear arc: new instruments and melodic variations every section, a warm lift near the end, spacious mix with room for a voice-over. Cinematic but intimate, tech documentary feel.
```

```text
[Instrumental]
[Intro: solo piano and soft harp, curious and gentle]
[Section A: pizzicato strings and flute introduce the main melody, light pulse]
[Section B: clarinet and cello answer, warm and steady, melody varies]
[Build: strings rise gradually, glockenspiel accents]
[Outro: warm full strings with soft brass, bright but restrained, resolves gently on a held chord]
```

## Track B — rising, high point, then focused

```text
Instrumental modern orchestral, uplifting, energetic but gentle, positive and confident. Plucked strings and marimba drive a flowing rhythm, violins carry a bright rising melody, French horn and soft trumpet add warmth. Light hand percussion, soft kick and shaker, 98 BPM, D major to G major. Builds steadily to a joyful peak about one third of the way through, holds it briefly, then settles into a calm, focused and transparent texture with harp, piano and clarinet for the second half. Constantly developing: different rhythmic patterns and counter-melodies in every section, no looping. Clean, open mix that leaves space for narration.
```

```text
[Instrumental]
[Intro: marimba and plucked strings, light and curious]
[Section A: violins enter with a rising theme, steady pulse]
[Build: layered strings, counter-melody in clarinet and horn, rising energy]
[Peak: bright full orchestra, joyful, warm and soft, not triumphant]
[Release: woodwinds and harp, calm and satisfied]
[Section B: piano arpeggios and pizzicato, focused, light and transparent]
[Outro: simple strings, gentle resolve]
```

## Track C — calm focus → warm finale with a long fade

```text
Instrumental modern cinematic orchestral, calm and focused at first, then uplifting, warm, hopeful and inspiring, with a feeling of arrival. Starts with fingerstyle harp, soft piano arpeggios, airy flute and warm clarinet over a light pulse, then grows through layered violins, French horns and light brass into a bright, heartfelt finale. Gentle acoustic guitar, glockenspiel, harp glissandi, subtle timpani and soft snare roll. 92 BPM, D major, with a modulation up in the last third. Compact arc of about two and three-quarter minutes, new melodic ideas in each section, never repetitive. Warm and optimistic, not dramatic. Ends with a long, graceful, resolved fade so the last narration line lands cleanly.
```

```text
[Instrumental]
[Intro: harp arpeggios and airy flute, calm and focused]
[Section A: piano and clarinet, light theme, low density]
[Build: violins and horns, timpani roll, growing warmth]
[Finale: full orchestra, bright and heartfelt, key lift]
[Outro: piano and harp, graceful resolve, long fade]
```
